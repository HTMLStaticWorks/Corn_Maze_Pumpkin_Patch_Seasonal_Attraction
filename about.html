Our Story

A Family Tradition

What started as a small pumpkin stall by the roadside has grown into the region's premier seasonal attraction.

Today, we manage over 200 acres of fertile farmland, hosting thousands of visitors every autumn. Our commitment to sustainability and education remains at the core of everything we do.
