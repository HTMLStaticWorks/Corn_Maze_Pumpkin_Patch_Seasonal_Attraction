Location

Finding Your Way

We're located just 20 minutes from downtown, tucked away in the beautiful rolling hills of Pumpkin Valley.

Parking Information

Free parking is available for all guests. We have dedicated spots for buses and disabled parking near the main entrance.

Driving Tips

The roads leading to the farm are scenic but winding. We recommend arriving at least 30 minutes before your scheduled hayride.

Quick Directions

  • 1

    Take I-95 North to Exit 42 (Harvest Road).

  • 2

    Turn right onto Pumpkin Valley Pkwy.

  • 3

    Drive 5 miles and follow the signs for "The Maze".
