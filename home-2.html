Carved by Precision

Every April, we sit down with our master designers to sketch out the year's theme. Using advanced GPS technology, we carve the paths when the corn is just 6 inches tall.

It's a blend of ancient farming wisdom and modern technology that creates the most challenging maze in the tri-state area.

Design Fun Facts:
  • Over 5 miles of actual walking path
  • 250,000+ individual corn stalks
  • GPS accuracy within 2 centimeters
