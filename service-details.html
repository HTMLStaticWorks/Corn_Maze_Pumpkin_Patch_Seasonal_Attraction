The Giant Corn Labyrinth

Experience the mystery of our 10-acre corn maze, meticulously designed every year to challenge even the most experienced navigators.

What to Expect

Checkpoints

Find all 5 hidden stations to collect stamps and win a harvest prize at the end.

Timed Runs

Competitive sessions on Saturday mornings with leaderboard tracking.

Safety First

Your safety is our priority. The maze is equipped with emergency exits every 200 yards, and our 'Maze Marshals' are always on patrol to assist if you get truly lost.

GPS-Guided Precision

Our maze isn't just planted; it's engineered. We use specialized GPS software to carve the intricate paths when the corn is still young.

Essential Visitor Tips

Stay Hydrated

The maze can get warm during the day. Carry a water bottle.

Wear Sunscreen

Even in autumn, the sun is strong in the open fields.
